Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Planora (planoraai.net) is a daily planner. This page explains, in plain words, what information Planora keeps, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the name you give (optional), and how you sign in (email and password, Google, or Apple). If you use Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • What you put into Planora: your tasks, events, goals, journal entries, study plans and the answers you give during onboarding (for example, your main goal and the time of day you work best).
  • Sign-in information: a secure sign-in session so you stay signed in on your device.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other websites, and we do not sell your information.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To save your plans so they are the same on all your devices.
  • To make plan suggestions when you ask Planora (Ask Planora). Planora only suggests; nothing in your schedule changes until you confirm it.
  • To send emails you need, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.

Planora does not make medical or psychological judgements about you and does not try to guess sensitive information from your journal.

Where it is stored and who helps us

  • Supabase stores your account and your Planora data, and handles sign-in. Each account's data can only be read by that account.
  • Google and Apple handle the sign-in step if you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple".
  • OpenAI: when you use Ask Planora, the text you type and the planning details it needs (such as your tasks and free times) may be sent to OpenAI to create a suggested plan. It is not used to show you ads.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Cloudflare (cdnjs) provides the icon font. Like any website, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

On your device

Planora keeps a copy of your plans and your sign-in session in your browser's storage so the app is fast and works offline. Logging out removes your Planora data from that device.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile → Privacy and data → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Privacy and data → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your Planora data.
  • Change your details: you can change your name, email and password in Profile.

Children

Planora is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
